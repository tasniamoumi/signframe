Teach your signs

The app learns each sign from examples of your own hands. Press Record, get into position during the countdown, then hold the sign and move your hands a little nearer, further and side to side. Record "Not a sign" while holding the frame and while your hands are relaxed in view.

SignExamples

How it works

1. Make a sign
Hold one of your taught two-hand signs for about half a second. The room behind you becomes that sign's world.
2. Make the frame
Hold up both hands with thumbs and index fingers out. A character appears between them.
3. Next character
Drop the frame and make it again.

Keyboard: 1 2 3 enter a world, 0 returns to the room, N next character, H shows or hides hand points.

Hand tracking and background cut-out by MediaPipe, running on your device. The characters and worlds are original to this project.
